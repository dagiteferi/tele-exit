# Tele-Exit — Website & product guide

## What is Tele-Exit?

Tele-Exit is a one-on-one live study experience with an AI study partner for final-year Ethiopian university students preparing for exit exams. You practice with previous-year exit exam questions in your field — talking through your thinking, getting explanations when stuck, and closing gaps before exam day.

Tagline from the site: Practice for your exit exam the way you'd study with a friend who really knows the material.

## Who it is for

Designed for final-year Ethiopian university students who need focused practice, not another pile of notes.

## Core benefits

- **Real questions, not summaries** — Every session is built from previous-year exit exam questions in your field — the same kind of material you'll face on exam day.
- **A conversation, not a quiz** — Talk through your reasoning out loud. The AI asks follow-ups, explains what's unclear, and can find a video when it helps.
- **Aimed at your exam date** — Your readiness score and weekly plan shift as your date gets closer so you work on what matters most next.

## How it works (three steps)

1. **Start a call** — Open a live one-on-one session when you're ready — no scheduling maze, just you and your study partner.
2. **Work through a real question together** — Talk through previous-year exit exam questions out loud. Ask for help when stuck; keep going when you're not.
3. **Get a readiness score and plan** — After each session, readiness updates and next focus areas become clearer.

## Accounts & signup

## Practice exams & study calls

- Browse exams for your department, open practice or exam mode.
- **Practice mode** shows answers and AI help; **exam mode** scores you without hints.
- Start a **study call** to work with the AI coach out loud (shared-screen style UX).
- You can choose an AI coach avatar and speaking voice in **Settings**.
- Progress saves your stop point so you can resume later. Profile and Dashboard show questions visited and resume cards.

## Readiness, progress & calendar

- Readiness updates from topic accuracy after scored practice / wrap-ups.
- Progress page shows sessions, coverage, and topic accuracy.
- After ending a session you get a recap with AI calendar recommendations; Accept can push to Google Calendar when credentials are configured.
- Send a progress report by email from Progress or Calendar flows.
- Report frequency (weekly / monthly) is controlled in Settings.
